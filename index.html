Ai Cá Ngựa

Play Vietnamese Ludo on iPhone and iPad with a Vietnamese-language interface. Known as cờ cá ngựa in Vietnam, the game uses horse pieces: roll the dice, capture opponents and race all four horses home. Play against the computer or invite friends with a room code or QR code. Choose Vietnamese or Ludo rules before a match.
