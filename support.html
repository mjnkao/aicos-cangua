Playing and connecting

Computer play requires no account or Internet connection. To play with friends, one person creates a room and others join using a room code or QR invitation. Internet rooms require a network connection; nearby play requires Local Network permission. Camera access is needed only when scanning QR codes.

Reporting a problem

Include the app version, device model and steps to reproduce the problem. Remove personal information from screenshots. Do not send passwords, verification codes or active room invitations.

In the app, open Settings (Cài đặt) → Feedback (Góp ý). If sending fails, the draft stays on your device so you can retry.

Personal data

To request access, correction or deletion, email us and specify Ai Cá Ngựa. See the privacy policy for the data involved.
